About Us

Celebrating our African roots, sharing our flavors with the world.

Why We Built This

Tastes of Africa was created to celebrate, protect, and share the rich culinary traditions of the African continent. We believe food is a meaningful way to explore and understand different cultures, and African cuisine carries unique stories and traditions that deserve to be shared with the world.

Our goal is to make African recipes available and easy to explore for everyone, whether they are people living abroad who want to reconnect with the flavours of home or food enthusiasts eager to experience African cuisine for the first time. We hope every visitor discovers a new ingredient, finds a dish they would love to prepare, and gains a greater appreciation of Africa's cultural and culinary diversity.

A Brief History of African Food Culture

African food is one of the oldest and most diverse food cultures in the world. Long before Africa became connected to other parts of the world through trade, African communities already had their own ways of growing food, preparing meals, and preserving ingredients.

In West Africa, people have grown crops such as sorghum, millet, and yams for thousands of years. Ancient kingdoms like Mali and Songhai depended on farming to support their communities. In North Africa, African and Arab cultures influenced each other, creating popular dishes like Moroccan couscous and tagine.

Along the East African coast, Swahili food was shaped by traders from Arabia, India, and Persia. This brought flavours like coconut, cardamom, and cloves into local cooking. In Ethiopia, people developed a special food culture around teff, a grain used to make the famous injera.

Today, African food is becoming more popular around the world. Chefs in Africa and other parts of the world are introducing traditional African dishes to new audiences while keeping their cultural roots alive.
